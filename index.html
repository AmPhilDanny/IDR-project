About IDR

The Institute of Digital Risk (IDR) is an industry-led training and deployment institute focused on preparing professionals for roles in digital, cyber, and technology risk.

Through strong collaboration with UK academic institutions and real-world industry partners, IDR supports learners working in high-consequence environments.

Our Model & Service Pillars

📚

Academy

Training and bootcamps for students and professionals in digital, AI, cyber and technology risk.

🧭

Innovation & Incubation

Developing research, IP, AI governance, and future risk models for high-consequence environments.

🛡️

Advisory Services

Helping organisations implement frameworks such as NIST, ISO 27001, and NIS2.
